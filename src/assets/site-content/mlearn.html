Overview

mLearn is a cross‑platform desktop application for Super-Fast Language Learning through immersion. Watch your favorite content in your target language—even as a complete beginner—using native media.

More information is available on [a href="https://mlearn.kikan.net"]mlearn.kikan.net[/a].

The app is source-available; you can find its source code [a href="https://github.com/adrianvla/mlearn"]here[/a].

Features

  • Interactive subtitles for any video — click any word for an instant definition
  • Knows what words you know — passively tracks every word you see and adjusts itself based on that
  • Built-in SRS flashcards that create themselves — with screenshot, audio, and context sentence (Anki-like algorithm)
  • Seamless two-way sync with Anki (AnkiConnect), if you prefer
  • Transparent subtitle overlay that works over any app — Netflix, YouTube, VLC, browsers
  • Browser extension (Chrome/Firefox) with native YouTube subtitle support
  • Streams any video source — local files, .mp4/.mkv, .m3u8 streams — plus video clipping
  • mLearn Reader: manga, PDFs and EPUBs with real-time OCR text recognition and automatic grammatical tokenization
  • AI tutor & explainer — runs locally offline (Gemma 4 LLM), via Ollama, or in the cloud; voice chat included
  • Voice cloning + TTS — make any character read your flashcards aloud
  • Smart vocabulary assessment (Word Sync) with weighted sampling
  • Language exam study modes — JLPT, HSK, CEFR & ТРКИ level tracking and vocabulary selection
  • Character knowledge grid (e.g., kanji) filtered by exam level
  • Known word blurring & Hardcore Mode (hide subtitles, hover to peek)
  • Pitch accent, reading aids and grammar-aware subtitle coloring
  • Watch Together — synced playback and subtitles with friends, no app needed on their end
  • Phone–desktop sync via QR code — data transfers directly between your devices
  • Statistics dashboard with heatmaps, streaks and immersion tracking
  • Access to an internal database to edit word definitions
  • Power-efficient and fully offline-capable — local AI, local OCR, local everything
  • Very customizable — 7 themes and extensive settings
  • And many more!!!

Supported languages

Japanese, German, Spanish, Russian, Mandarin Chinese, and Church Slavonic are fully supported out of the box — with per-language frequency and exam levels (JLPT, HSK, CEFR/ТРКИ). You can also add custom languages.

Why I built this

I began developing mLearn in Summer/Autumn 2024 after starting Japanese in February 2024. Native media were inaccessible at a beginner level.

I needed an app that enabled immersion with any content—including offline sources—with Anki integration. I tried [a href="https://migaku.com/"]Migaku[/a], but it was not free and did not support my goals for native‑level content.

That motivated me to build a complete language learning app focused on immersion, even for beginners.

Because I could not understand most Japanese at the time, I created a live word translator that appends translations based on readings.

It helped significantly: the first series I watched using mLearn was [a href="https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2098220/"]Hunter x Hunter[/a] with Japanese subtitles and the original dub.

mLearn helped me reach conversational fluency. I learned over 1,000 words with minimal friction, as I later discovered in an internal database.

As of Summer 2026, mLearn helped me learn so many words passively that I finished a 6k word Anki deck almost without friction. mLearn reports that I know 5.8k words, and 8.9k additional words are almost mature in my vocabulary.
